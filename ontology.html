recommends recommends

A song to listen to next after another.

From
Song
To
Song
Characteristics
irreflexive
Weight
1 / rank
Evidence
One reason per meta-path that relates the songs
Rule
Relevance: the sum over meta-paths (sounds like, sounds like estimated, same first release) of path weight x path score, scaled to the song's best, minus the hidden gem nudge. Credits are not used. Five songs are picked one at a time by MMR (VarietyLambda). Ties go to the song that sounds closer, then the less streamed (streams per day), then the earlier track on the song's first release. Skits are neither recommended nor given recommendations.

Sounds like SoundsLike

The sounds_like similarity, against the nearest for the song. The reason gives tempo, energy and speechiness: the recommended song's, then this song's.

Route
song -sounds_like- song
Weight
1.0
Reason label
sounds like

Sounds like (estimated) SoundsLikeEstimated

The sounds_like_estimated similarity, against the nearest for the song. Connects songs with only estimated audio to the catalog. The reason gives tempo and energy: the recommended song's, then this song's.

Route
song -sounds_like_estimated- song
Weight
0.5
Reason label
sounds like (estimated)

Settings

Hidden gem weight HiddenGemWeight

Relevance is lowered by this times the song's popularity percentile / 100, so a much-streamed song needs a closer match to be picked.

Value
0.1

Variety lambda VarietyLambda

MMR: each pick maximizes lambda x relevance - (1 - lambda) x its similarity to songs already picked (audio closeness, or 1 for the same first release). 1 = relevance only.

Value
0.7
